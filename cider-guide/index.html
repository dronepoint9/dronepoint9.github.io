Cider Solar Field Guide

Weekly capture, M4E wide, ~470 ft AGL. Tap an area or a TOL.
TOL in or next to area Chained from another TOL Daisy chain Support area Captured TOL Base
Launch points come from the TOLs in the Cider Solar Final KML. Daisy-chained flights follow the standing launch rules (3+5+4 from 04, 24+25E+25W from 25W, 15W+15E from 15E, 2E+2W from 02E, 19+SS from the Substation TOL, 14+13 from 14, 21+20 from 21, 9+10 from 10, 7+8 from 08, Off+26+27 from Base). Acreage is computed from the polygons. Capture order is the standard single-pilot full-site order; air times are typical M4E minutes from logs 26-014, 26-019 and 26-031. Checkmarks are saved on this phone only. In numerical view, checking an area checks its whole flight, including chained arrays and both batteries.
